# AQUAVEIN-SAR — Unveiling Hidden Groundwater Pathways

**AQUAVEIN-SAR** is a satellite-based exploration and visualization platform developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge Niğde. The project focuses on investigating Submarine Groundwater Discharge (SGD), the process through which groundwater flows from coastal aquifers into the ocean.

By exploring Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) satellite observations and environmental data, the project aims to investigate potential groundwater interaction zones along coastal areas.

The platform provides an interactive 3D Earth model, satellite visualizations, map-based exploration areas, and digital scientific content to make complex environmental processes easier to understand and explore.

## Challenge

**Dancing with SAR**

Explore the use of Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) technology in Earth observation and investigate its potential contribution to understanding environmental processes through satellite radar data.

## Data Sources and Research Infrastructure

### NASA Data Sources

- **NASA NISAR Mission:** https://nisar.jpl.nasa.gov/ — A reference source for L-band and S-band SAR technology, surface changes, and soil moisture research.
- **NASA Earthdata:** https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/ — NASA's main portal for discovering and accessing Earth observation datasets.
- **NISAR Data Products:** https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/data/ — Information about NISAR satellite data and data products.
- **NISAR Sample Data:** https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/sample-data/ — Sample resources for exploring SAR data products and their processing.
- **NASA SWOT Mission:** https://podaac.jpl.nasa.gov/SWOT — Satellite observations of ocean surface height and surface water, including coastal areas.
- **NASA NISAR Soil Moisture:** https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/nisar-provisional-soil-moisture-version-1 — Surface soil moisture products derived from satellite radar observations.

These resources support the scientific background of the project and help explore the potential of SAR technology and other Earth observation datasets. NISAR, SWOT, and soil moisture products can provide complementary information for environmental research. However, none of these satellite products directly images freshwater beneath the seafloor.
